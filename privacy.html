About & privacy

What MathAnchor is

MathAnchor is a free website for students who are stuck on a math topic. Each topic has a plain-language explanation, a worked example, a common mistake to avoid and three practice questions, from arithmetic up to graduate-level subjects.

Lessons are written to explain, not to replace a teacher or textbook. They may contain mistakes. If something looks wrong, use the “Report a mistake” button on the lesson and it will open an email to us.

Your data

When you sign up we store your email address, username, profile picture and learning progress (completed lessons, streak and last lesson) with our account provider, Supabase. Passwords are never stored in readable form. This lets your progress follow you to other devices and lets you reset a forgotten password by email. We only use this data to run your account.

Your theme and language choices are saved in your own browser. You can delete your account at any time in Settings; this removes your account, email and progress from our servers.

We do not run advertising or analytics on these pages. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when a page loads.

Languages

The menus and buttons are available in 9 languages (English, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Chinese, Hindi and French). Lessons from Pre-algebra through Calculus are translated into all of them; a few topics are still English only. The translations were made by machine and have not yet been reviewed by native speakers, so some wording may be off.
